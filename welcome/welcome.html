    Questions

    Is it free?

    Yes. There's nothing to buy.

    Do I need an account?

    No. You can try it without one, and your plan saves on that device. An account lets you sync between your phone and PC and join the leaderboard.

    What games does it work with?

    Any game. Shooters, MOBAs, fighting games, chess, GeoGuessr, racing, rhythm games, Minecraft, and more. If it doesn't know your game, it uses a general practice plan.

    Does it work offline?

    Yes, once you've opened it. Install it from Settings to use it like a normal app.

    What do you do with my data?

    Your planner is only readable by you. We don't sell data or show ads. Read the privacy policy for the details.
