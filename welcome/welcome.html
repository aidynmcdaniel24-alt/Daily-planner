How it works

From sign-up to your first checked-off day in about a minute.

  1. 1

    Answer a few questions

    Pick quick setup (4 questions) or full setup for a more detailed plan. You'll be asked things like:

    • What game you play
    • How good you are now
    • What you want to fix
    • Which days you're free
    • Your coding level
    • Your bedtime
  2. 2

    Get your plan

    You see the whole plan before you start, and you can change any of it.

    • Free days get a longer routine, busy days get a quick one
    • Practice drills made for your game, with step-by-step guides
    • A bedtime routine with a 7+ hour sleep goal
    • A coding course pick and a new project idea every day
  3. 3

    Check it off each day

    Your checklists start fresh each day at midnight.

    • Finish a whole list to grow that streak
    • Log calm or tilted sessions, and take a break when the planner suggests one
    • Track your scores, rank, and sleep over time
    • Earn badges and take on 3 new challenges every week
  4. 4

    Change it as you go

    Switch games, add or reorder tasks, change your free days or bedtime, or redo setup from Settings whenever your schedule changes.

    Getting started

    Is it free?

    Yes, everything is free and there's nothing to buy. AI features have a daily limit per device so the app stays free for everyone.

    Do I need an account?

    No. You can try it without one, and your plan saves on that device. An account lets you sync between your phone and PC, join the leaderboard, and keep your data if you switch devices.

    What games does it work with?

    Any game. It has built-in plans for shooters, MOBAs, fighting games, chess, GeoGuessr, racing, rhythm games, Minecraft, and more. For other games, AI builds a plan from the name you type.

    Can I use it on my phone?

    Yes. On Android, open it in Chrome and choose Install app. On iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It then opens like a normal app and works offline.

    Using the planner

    How do streaks work?

    Each checklist has its own streak. Finish every task on a list in one day and that streak goes up by one. Miss a day and it starts over.

    How does the AI plan work?

    When you type your game, AI writes drills, score labels, and rank names for it. Only the game name and your coding level are sent, never your name or email. AI can make mistakes, so you can edit any task.

    Will it remind me?

    Reminders show while the planner is open. To get alerts when it's closed, use Add reminders to my phone's calendar in Settings.

    How does the leaderboard work?

    Join from your profile menu and share your 6-letter friend code. Friends you add see your leaderboard name and streaks, nothing else. You can nudge a friend once a day.

    Privacy and your account

    What do you do with my data?

    Your planner can only be read by you. We don't sell data, show ads, or use tracking cookies. Read the privacy policy for the details.

    How do I keep my account safe?

    Use a password you don't use anywhere else. Settings shows your recent logins, and Log out of all devices signs you out everywhere if something looks wrong.

    How do I delete my account?

    Go to Settings and choose Delete account. Your account, planner, login history, and leaderboard entry are removed for good. Export your data first if you want a copy.

    I found a bug. Where do I report it?

    Use Send feedback in your profile menu, or report it on GitHub.
