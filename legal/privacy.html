Privacy Policy

Last updated October 3, 2026

Daily Planner is a free app made by one independent developer. This page explains, in plain words, what the app saves and why.

What we save

  • If you use it without an account: everything stays on your device in your browser's storage. Nothing is sent to us.
  • If you make an account: your email address, the name you type, and your planner data (checklists, logs, notes, scores, ranks, and settings) are saved so they sync across your devices.
  • Recent logins: the type of device and browser (like "Chrome on Windows") and the time you logged in, so you can spot logins that weren't you.
  • Leaderboard (only if you join): your leaderboard name, friend code, and streak numbers.
  • Feedback (only if you send it): your message and which page you were on.

AI features

To make a practice plan for your game and daily coding project ideas, the app sends the game name you type, your coding level, and the type of coding you picked to Google's Gemini AI (through Google Firebase). Your name, email, and planner data are not sent. On Google's free tier, Google may use these requests to improve its products. AI can make mistakes, so double-check tips that seem off.

Who can see it

  • Your planner data, notes, and logins can only be read by you.
  • Friends you add on the leaderboard can see your leaderboard name and streaks. Other signed-in users who know your friend code can see the same.
  • The developer can read feedback you send. The developer can technically access stored account data through Firebase, but doesn't look at your planner data.

Where it's stored

Account data is stored with Google Firebase, which runs on Google's servers. Google Firebase also handles sign-in. Google reCAPTCHA is used to block bots, and fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Bible verse text is loaded from bible-api.com, which sees your IP address like any website. Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't use tracking or analytics cookies.

Deleting your data

Go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, planner data, login history, and leaderboard entry. Feedback you sent is kept unless you ask for it to be removed. Guest data can be cleared by clearing your browser's site data.

Age

You must be 13 or older to make an account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change, and big changes will show in the app's "What's new" popup.

Contact

Questions? Reach out by opening an issue on GitHub or using Send feedback in the app.
